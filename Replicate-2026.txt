1. Satellite Data
Sentinel-1 (SAR Data)
* Sensor Type: Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR)
* Acquisition Mode: Typically IW (Interferometric Wide Swath)
* Spatial Resolution: 10 meters
* Key Characteristics:
   * Operates in C-band microwave, enabling all-weather and day–night imaging
   * Sensitive to surface roughness and moisture variations
   * Useful for detecting terrain deformation 
Sentinel-2 (Multispectral Data)
* Sensor Type: Optical multispectral imaging
* Bands: 13 spectral bands (Visible, NIR, SWIR)
* Spatial Resolution:
   * 10 m (Bands 2, 3, 4, 8)
   * 20 m (Bands 5, 6, 7, 8A, 11, 12)
   * 60 m (Bands 1, 9, 10)
* Key Characteristics:
   * Provides rich spectral information for vegetation, soil, and land cover analysis
   * Enables computation of indices such as NDVI, NDWI, and NBR
   * Supports detection of vegetation loss and exposed soil in landslide regions
